### 3，几个重要的类

##### 1) GameObject

![](Pasted%20image%2020260620125209.png)

SetActive停止活动状态

**GameObject.CreatePrimitive(PrimitiveType.type)**
创建预制体
如
``` Csharp
for(int i=0;i<50;i++){
	GameObject  go=GameObject.
	  CreatePrimitive(Primitive.Sphere);
	go.transform.position = new Vector3(i,0,0);
}//创建50个球体并排成一排，go是临时变量，但只是引用
```
